Your allocation

You can sell only free (unlocked) tokens, up to the per-window limit, while the price is above the gate. Proceeds are paid in USDT; a 10% fee (in USDT) applies.

Sell & withdraw

Your KAVI is sold on PancakeSwap for USDT. You receive USDT minus the 10% fee (also in USDT).

Deposit KAVI

Move KAVI into the contract so allocations are backed. This approves the token, then deposits.

Propose allocation

Assign new tokens to a user from the unallocated pool. Both approvers must sign before you execute.

Propose reallocation

Move an existing user's tokens to another user. Needs both wallets' approval. 10% of the amount is burned; the recipient receives the rest.

Propose config change

Every parameter change needs both approvers before it can execute.

Open requests

Sign or execute anything waiting on you. Buttons show only when your wallet is a party.
